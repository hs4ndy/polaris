Privacy Policy

Draft prepared October 4, 2026

This policy is awaiting launch review. The operator's public contact details, applicable legal basis, and hosting-log retention must be confirmed before this becomes the final notice.

Polaris is an independent tracker for the Infinite Flight simulator. This draft describes the current website implementation, not every practice of the services it connects to.

Simulator flight information

Polaris obtains flight information from the Infinite Flight Live API to display flights, routes, and aircraft details. This can include a pilot's simulator username, callsign, virtual organization, aircraft and livery, simulator position, speed, altitude, flight plan, and route history. Simulator positions are not measurements of a visitor's physical location.

Technical connection information

Your browser connects to our hosting providers and flight-data proxy. These services receive technical request information such as IP addresses and request headers. The proxy uses IP addresses for temporary rate-limit and connection counters to help protect the service from abuse.

How information is used

Flight information is used to operate the tracker and its search, route, and history features. Technical connection information is used to deliver the site, maintain availability, and limit abusive requests. The current Polaris application has no user registration, payment form, advertising integration, or visitor analytics script.

Service providers

The site is hosted on Vercel and the flight-data proxy runs on Render. The tracker also requests map resources from CARTO and OpenStreetMap-based services, fonts from Google Fonts, and MapLibre software from unpkg. Those providers receive the requests made to their services and handle information under their own policies. Aircraft photos are served as bundled site assets.

Storage and retention

The proxy keeps active-flight information and recent route samples in process memory. Recent route samples are capped at 120 points per flight, and a flight's local route history is removed when it leaves the current feed. The tracker can also request earlier route points from Infinite Flight. Browser flight data is held in page memory. Hosting and upstream providers may retain operational logs or simulator records separately; their retention periods have not yet been confirmed for this notice.

Cookies and browser storage

The current Polaris source does not set application cookies or use localStorage or sessionStorage. That does not establish that every hosting or third-party service uses no cookies. See the cookie information for the current review status.

Questions and privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of your personal information. A public contact channel for the Polaris operator must be added before launch. Requests concerning original Infinite Flight account or simulator records may also need to be directed to Infinite Flight.

Updates

This notice will be reviewed when Polaris changes its features, providers, or data practices.
