Cookie Information

Draft prepared October 4, 2026

The hosting and third-party cookie audit is not complete. This page is a source-code-based draft, not a claim that the entire site is cookie-free.

What Polaris currently uses

The current Polaris application does not explicitly set cookies or use localStorage or sessionStorage. Search selections and flight data are held in page memory. The application does not include advertising or visitor analytics scripts.

External services

Hosting, map, font, and software delivery services receive browser requests. Any cookies or similar technologies associated with those services must be checked separately before launch. Browser caching of ordinary website assets also helps pages load efficiently.

Consent and preferences

Polaris has not added a cosmetic cookie-acceptance banner. Before introducing non-essential tracking that requires consent under applicable law, the site must provide an appropriate choice and prevent that tracking until consent is given. If a consent mechanism becomes necessary, it must also let visitors withdraw their choice.

Your browser controls

You can review and remove stored site data using your browser's privacy settings. Blocking certain resources may affect maps, fonts, or other features.

More information

See the Privacy Policy for information about simulator data and technical connections. A public operator contact channel must be added before launch.
